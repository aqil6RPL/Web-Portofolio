About Me

Hi, I’m a student at SMK IDN Boarding School Solo, currently taking the Software Engineering program. I enjoy coding because it gives me the freedom to express my creativity and turn my ideas into something real. For me, programming is not just about writing code, but about creating projects that reflect my imagination and passion.

My Hobby

I love spending my free time exploring different hobbies. Playing games and making my own creations on Roblox give me the chance to be creative and have fun. I also enjoy drawing, which helps me express my imagination in a visual way. Besides that, I like playing badminton because it keeps me active and motivated. For me, these hobbies are more than just activities — they’re ways to grow, learn, and enjoy life.

My Strength

My biggest strength is in coding. I can pick up programming concepts quickly and easily, which makes learning new languages and tools exciting for me. Because of this, I can turn ideas into real projects faster and enjoy solving problems along the way. This ability helps me stay creative, adaptable, and ready to grow as a developer.

My Wikness

Even though I’m good at coding, I know I have some weaknesses. School subjects outside of IT can be tough for me, especially when it comes to memorization. I also find it challenging to memorize the Qur’an, and being a bit introverted sometimes makes it harder to connect with people. Still, I believe these weaknesses can help me grow, and I’m trying to improve by practicing more, learning better study habits, and slowly becoming more confident in social situations.
